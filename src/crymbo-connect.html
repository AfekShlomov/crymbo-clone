Use Cases

Crymbo Pay - Payments

Crymbo Pay integrates TradFi, CeFi, and DeFi into a single API, simplifying payment processes and expanding settlement options with digital assets. Offering fast execution, robust security, and full compliance with KYT and KYC requirements, Crymbo enables businesses to scale efficiently.

Crymbo Broker - Trading

Crymbo simplifies access to crypto markets through seamless integration with OTC platforms and white-label trading solutions. Featuring advanced orchestration and full compliance, it delivers flexibility, security, and a tailored experience for financial platforms.

Crymbo Portfolio - Illiquid token management

Seamlessly manage your liquid and illiquid assets with built-in risk management and advanced orchestration. Consolidate everything in one platform, designed to simplify trading and streamline your portfolio management.

THE PROBLEM

Crypto infrastructure is fragmented

making it hard to talk to each other leaving you open to risk breaches, siloed data and manual processes held together by humans.

You can't build a compliant, scalable, digital assets business for onchain and fiat assets on fragmented infrastructure.

Combining CeFi, DeFi with our TradFi businesses require manual integrations, spreadsheets and teams of people to stitch data together.

Crypto market structure is constantly shifting. Pivoting & scaling crypto infrastructure can be slow, expensive and risky.
