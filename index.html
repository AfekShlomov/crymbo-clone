Identity

By leveraging cryptographically secure identity binding, Crymbo enables financial institutions to meet regulatory compliance requirements across DeFi, CeFi, and TradFi ecosystems. This system ensures privacy-preserving compliance, reducing costs and risks while unlocking access to permissionless blockchain liquidity.

PII Handling

Crymbo Oracle prioritizes privacy by ensuring Personally Identifiable Information (PII) is never shared openly across the blockchain. No entity, including Crymbo itself, can access your PII. The system encrypts each partner's data differently, safeguarding sensitive information and minimizing security risks.

Auditable

The Crymbo Explorer provides a powerful tool for auditable transparency. It enables transaction references and compliance data to be easily searchable, ensuring streamlined verification while maintaining user privacy. This supports regulatory oversight without compromising decentralized principles.

Localised

Crymbo Oracle is designed to adapt to local regulations, enabling institutions to operate within specific regional compliance frameworks. This localized approach ensures Crymbo remains flexible and compliant in diverse global markets, supporting adoption and operational efficiency.
